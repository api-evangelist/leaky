# Leaky

> Leaky was a US auto-insurance comparison service that operated from 2011 until
> 31 August 2013. It let consumers compare prices and plans across the nation's
> top insurers, model coverage scenarios, and monitor their policies for savings.
> The comparison engine was built by reverse-engineering the pricing models of
> more than 100 insurers from publicly-available government filings. Leaky ceased
> auto-insurance operations on 2013-08-31 and its commercial-insurance business
> was acquired by Navion Insurance. The company was backed by 500 Global
> (500 Startups).

## Status

- DEFUNCT. leaky.com serves a single static shutdown notice; all other paths return 404.
- No developer portal, no public API, no SDKs, no documentation, and no /.well-known/ surface exist.
- All user data and account information was deleted at shutdown.
- The commercial.leaky.com property linked from the shutdown page no longer resolves.

## Reported outcomes (as published on the shutdown notice)

- Over $300 million of auto-insurance quotes provided
- Over $40 million saved for users
- Over 8 years of user time saved comparing policies
- A cease-and-desist received roughly every two months, on average
